## 6. Marketing & Finance
- **Marketing Budget:** ₹297M

- **Marketing ROI:** 248%

- **Top Channels:** Google Ads, Meta, Affiliate  

**Interpretation:** Marketing campaigns are highly profitable, but refunds due to delivery and quality issues erode gains.

---

## - Strategic Recommendations

### Operations & Logistics
- Partner with reliable couriers (Delhivery, BlueDart).  
- Reduce average delivery time from 5 days to 3 days.  
- Implement predictive delay alerts for customers.  

### Inventory Management
- Use demand forecasting to balance stock.  
- Restock fast‑moving products quickly.  
- Phase out slow‑moving Apex products.  

### Customer Experience
- Launch loyalty programs for Premium/VIP customers.  
- Improve product descriptions and sizing guides.  
- Communicate proactively about delays.  

### Marketing & Finance
- Continue investing in Google Ads and Meta.  
- Reduce reliance on discounts; focus on value campaigns.  
- Investigate refund causes with stricter quality checks.  

---

## - Final Takeaway
The company is in a high‑growth phase with strong revenue and marketing ROI. However, delivery delays, inventory mismanagement, and high refunds are eroding profitability. Addressing these operational challenges will unlock sustainable growth and protect margins.
